# MonitorEO

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23814894.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23814894)

*[See English version below](#english)*

MonitorEO-OBSNEV es una herramienta abierta de monitorización de Variables Esenciales de la Biodiversidad basada en sensores satelitales y modelos generados por la Universidad de Granada. Permite a la ciudadanía, gestores y científicos crear mapas y gráficos de métricas clave de los ecosistemas para apoyar la toma de decisiones.

**Documentación: [wiki](https://github.com/monitoreo-obsnev/monitoreo/wiki)**: instalación, conceptos, el manual de cada
herramienta con ejemplos, problemas frecuentes y [desarrollo](https://github.com/monitoreo-obsnev/monitoreo/wiki/Desarrollo).
Cada página tiene su versión en inglés.

**Novedades: [changelog](#changelog)**, lo que trae cada versión, al final de esta página (en inglés).

Todas calculan con Google Earth Engine y usan **las mismas funciones** (`monitoreo.services`): el mismo cálculo da el
mismo resultado en todas. Sus textos están en inglés; al asistente de IA se le puede preguntar en español y
responde en el idioma de la pregunta.

## Changelog

What each version brings, newest first. A version is that of the QGIS plugin, the tag of its
[release](https://github.com/monitoreo-obsnev/monitoreo/releases); the Python and R package `monitoreo` has its own
number, next to it. **All tools** are those that MonitorEO had at that version, and **(fix)** marks an error that was
fixed. **Unreleased** is what is already in `main` but not yet in the QGIS plugin nor in the Earth Engine app, which
get it only with a release: the assistant and the MCP server update themselves from `main` when they start, and the
Python and R packages installed from `main` have it too. It goes away with the next release of the QGIS plugin and the
Earth Engine app, which gives it its version and date.

### Unreleased

- **Python package, R package, Command line, MCP server, Assistant**: The recovery after a disturbance on each pixel (`recovery_map`; `monitoreo recovery-map`; `mo_recovery_map()`): the metric of each year of a period, within a season, and on each pixel the metrics of Marcos et al. (2023) of its annual series (reference, impact, DIT, RRT, the recovery time, RET and the share recovered), a GeoTIFF of a band each and another of a band per year. Not in the QGIS plugin nor the Earth Engine app.
- **Python package, R package, Command line, MCP server, Assistant, Earth Engine app**: A control ring around an affected area (`control_ring`; `monitoreo ring`; `mo_control_ring()`), as the EVEREST case studies built it: the land from 150 to 1,650 m of it (gap and width can change), without other disturbances (`exclude`) and only inside a region (`within`), with its pixels and those of the affected area matched by elevation (percentiles 5 and 95 in the affected area), written to a GeoPackage; and the option `ring` of the time series and the recovery metrics (`--ring`; a check box in the Earth Engine app): the series of the affected area, of its ring and of their difference. The QGIS plugin does not have it yet.

### [v0.3.82](https://github.com/monitoreo-obsnev/monitoreo/releases/tag/v0.3.82) · 2026-11-27 · package 0.3.92

- **All tools**: The Tasseled Cap of Landsat in the theme Vegetation: brightness, greenness and wetness (TCB, TCG and TCW), with the coefficients of Crist (1985) on the surface reflectance of Landsat 5, 7, 8 and 9, as the studies of forest disturbance and recovery with Landsat time series use them; with quality `harmonize` for the series that cross 2013.
- **Python package, R package, Command line, MCP server, Assistant, Earth Engine app**: The severity and recovery after a disturbance (`recovery_metrics`; `monitoreo recovery`; `mo_recovery_metrics()`; the section Recovery after a disturbance of the Earth Engine app): the metrics of the post-fire trajectories of Marcos et al. (2023) of the time series of an affected area and, with a control area, of the control and of their difference (BACI), on a robust STL decomposition (Cleveland et al. 1990): the severity S95 and the impact, the times DIT, RRT (the recovery time) and RET, the share of the loss recovered, the anomalies after the event and the trends before and after it. The QGIS plugin does not have it yet.

### [v0.3.81](https://github.com/monitoreo-obsnev/monitoreo/releases/tag/v0.3.81) · 2026-11-27 · package 0.3.91

- **Python package, R package, Command line, MCP server, Assistant**: The images and valid observations of a variable in each year, month or 16 days of a period over a region, after the pixel filters (`image_count`; `monitoreo count`; `mo_image_count()`): the images of each step, the valid images per pixel and the fraction of the region with data, to choose the period of a series or a trend before computing it (e.g. the summers of Landsat 5 in the 1990s). The QGIS plugin and the Earth Engine app give the same with the metric count of their time series.
- **All tools**: The burn severity keeps in the perimeter only the burned patch of the fire, the one nearest to a point in it (`seed`; `--seed LON LAT`; Keep in the QGIS plugin, Patches in the Earth Engine app) or the largest one (`largest`; `--largest`), so that a large region gives the perimeter of the fire without the other patches that pass the threshold (other fires, harvests, pastures that dried).
- **All tools**: The burn severity subtracts from the dNBR the median dNBR of a region of unburned land near the fire (`offset_region`; `--offset-region`; Offset region in the QGIS plugin, a layer of polygons; the button «Use this region as the offset region» in the Earth Engine app), before its classes, the RBR and the RdNBR, as the offset of Miller & Thode (2007) and Parks et al. (2014): the change between both periods that is not the fire (phenology, drought, the drying of pastures and heath) is no longer taken for burned land.
- **All tools** (fix): A time series with the statistic count over several steps (months, years) had no values; with one step it had them.
- **All tools** (fix): With a minimum coverage, the coverage of a step with the statistic mean or median could be a little more than 1 (the pixels that the region only touches were counted in the step but not in the region).

### [v0.3.80](https://github.com/monitoreo-obsnev/monitoreo/releases/tag/v0.3.80) · 2026-11-27 · package 0.3.90

- **All tools**: Landsat 5 and 7 harmonized with Landsat 8 and 9, an option off by default (quality `harmonize`; `--harmonize` in the command line; a check box in the QGIS plugin and the Earth Engine app): their surface reflectance becomes that of Landsat 8 and 9 with the functions of Roy et al. (2016), so that a long series or trend of Landsat has no step in 2013.
- **All tools**: Landsat 7 after 2003, an option off by default (quality `landsat7`; `--landsat7`; a check box): its images after the failure of its scan line corrector, with stripes without data, add images to 2003-2024 and give data from 2012-05-05 to 2013-03-18, between Landsat 5 and Landsat 8, a period that without it is refused.
- **All tools**: The minimum coverage of the steps of the time series, their comparison, the trend table and the export of the series (`min_coverage`, e.g. 0.5; `--min-coverage`; a check box of 50 % in the QGIS plugin and the Earth Engine app): the steps whose valid pixels are less than that fraction of the region have no value, and the rows say the coverage of each step.

### [v0.3.79](https://github.com/monitoreo-obsnev/monitoreo/releases/tag/v0.3.79) · 2026-11-27 · package 0.3.89

- **All tools**: Two new indices of Sentinel-2 and Landsat: the moisture stress index MSI (SWIR1 / NIR, higher is drier; Hunt & Rock 1989), in the theme Water, and the modified soil adjusted vegetation index MSAVI2 (Qi et al. 1994), for sparse vegetation, drylands and burned areas, in the theme Vegetation.
- **All tools**: The NDWI and the LSWI of MODIS from its 8-day surface reflectance at 500 m, of Terra (MOD09A1, since 2000) and Aqua (MYD09A1, since 2002), with the pixel filters of its quality band; the NDWI of MODIS of the catalog until now ends in February 2023 and stays as it was.
- **All tools**: The yearly gross and net primary production of MODIS (GPP and NPP of MOD17A3HGF and MYD17A3HGF, 500 m, 2001-2025 (Aqua since 2003), in kg of carbon per m² and year, without their fill values), in the theme Vegetation.
- **All tools**: Drought indices in the theme Climate: the Palmer Drought Severity Index of TerraClimate (PDSI, monthly, 4.6 km) and the SPEI of 1, 3, 6 and 12 months of SPEIbase v2.11 of the CSIC (SPEI1, SPEI3, SPEI6, SPEI12, monthly, 0.5°, 1901-2024).
- **All tools**: The burn date, the day of the year when each pixel burned (BURNDATE, only the burned pixels, the first day of the period), from MODIS MCD64A1 (500 m, since 2000) or ESA FireCCI51 (250 m, 2001-2020), in the theme Fire.
- **All tools**: The aspect of the Copernicus DEM (ASPECT, degrees from the north, without the flat pixels), a fixed layer of the theme Terrain; as a condition of «only the pixels where», a minimum greater than the maximum goes across the north (e.g. 315 to 45: the slopes that face north), and on big pixels it is their circular mean.

### [v0.3.76](https://github.com/monitoreo-obsnev/monitoreo/releases/tag/v0.3.76) · 2026-11-27 · package 0.3.86

- **Assistant, Command line**: New chat for managers (`monitoreo assistant`): an open language model on this computer helps choose variable, place and period; MonitorEO computes and makes a printable report.

- **Assistant**: Results open on a large map with basemaps, each year or month, pixel values and series, significant-only trend maps, and views added to the report.
- **Assistant**: Answers questions about MonitorEO from the wiki, takes uploaded study areas, downloads GeoTIFFs, exports to Google Drive and installs new versions from GitHub.
- **QGIS plugin, Assistant, MCP server, Python package, R package** (fix): Maps of big areas or long periods no longer fail when Earth Engine runs out of memory for their colour range; the catalog's range is used.

### [0.1](https://github.com/monitoreo-obsnev/monitoreo/releases/tag/0.1) · 2026-10-28 · package 0.1.0

- **All tools**: Thirteen satellite variables of the MonitorEO catalog (NDVI, EVI, NDWI, albedo, surface temperature, evapotranspiration, LAI and more), in 32 versions from Sentinel-2, Landsat and MODIS.
- **All tools**: Pixel filters for clouds, water, snow and aerosols are on by default, with Cloud Score+ for the clouds of Sentinel-2.
- **All tools**: Time series (table, CSV and chart), map layers, region statistics and Google Drive exports over protected areas, ecoregions, boxes, points or vector files.
- **QGIS plugin, MCP server, Python package, Command line, R package**: Available as a Python package with a command line, an MCP server for language models, an R package and a plugin for QGIS 3 and 4.
